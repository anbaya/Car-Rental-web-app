Common questions

The short version — the full list lives on the FAQ page.

What documents do I need to rent a car?

A valid driver's licence held for at least one year, your passport, and a card or cash deposit at pickup.

Is airport pickup really available at any hour?

Yes — send your flight number in the request and someone will be waiting at arrivals, whatever time you land.

Can I rent for just one day?

Yes, single-day rentals are available on most of the fleet, though a few vehicles carry a two-day minimum.

What happens to my deposit?

It's held as a hold on your card or in cash, and released in full at drop-off once the car's checked over.
